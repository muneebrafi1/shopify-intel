> **How this was written.** From our scan of 17 November 2026 and our captures of 25 November
> 2026 (home, collection and product pages, desktop and mobile) of thesill.com. Apps are matched
> to sections from the page code; where an app could render in more than one place, we say so.

## Theme

thesill.com runs [Horizon](/themes/horizon) **3.2.0**, Shopify's own theme, free on the Shopify
Theme Store. Horizon was detected on 14 of the 930 stores in the same scan, second only to Dawn
among themes we have a profile for. thesill.com is the only one of the 14 where the scan also read
a version number.

## Apps

The scan detected nine apps, grouped here by category:

- **Email & SMS:** [Klaviyo](/apps/klaviyo) and Powerful Contact Form Builder
- **Reviews:** [Stamped](/apps/stamped)
- **Subscriptions:** [Recharge](/apps/recharge)
- **Upsell & Bundles:** Monk Free Gift with Purchase and Giftnote
- **Video & UGC:** [Instafeed](/apps/instafeed)
- **Wishlist & Back in Stock:** Swym Wishlist Plus
- **Chat & Support:** [Gorgias](/apps/helpdesk)

Klaviyo was the most detected app in the scan (383 of 930 stores) and Instafeed the third (157).
The rest are rarer: Monk and Giftnote were found on three and two stores.

> A free theme, and nine apps doing the work around it: subscriptions, reviews, gifting, a
> wishlist and an Instagram feed.

## Tracking

Two tracking tags were detected: **Google Analytics 4** and **Google Tag Manager**.

## What to learn from it

1. Which app categories a store adds on top of a free Shopify theme. Here: subscriptions,
   reviews, gifting, a wishlist and a social feed, with chat and email behind them.
2. How many apps compete for the product page. Recharge and Swym both target the buy box, and
   the capture puts Stamped and Monk below it: four of the nine on one page.
3. Gifting split across two apps: Monk for free gifts and Giftnote for messages and cards.

## Open questions

1. How Recharge's subscribe option and the Swym wishlist share the buy box on desktop and mobile.
2. Whether Stamped reviews show photos, and whether they also appear on the home page.
3. Where Monk and Giftnote appear: the product page, the cart, or both.
4. Whether the Instafeed gallery sits on the home page only.
