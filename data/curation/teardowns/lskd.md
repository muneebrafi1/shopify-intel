> **Note.** Written from our 17 Nov 2026 scan of 930 Shopify stores (theme, detected apps, tracking tags) and Shopify App Store / Theme Store records. No revenue, traffic or conversion estimates are used.

## Theme

lskd.co runs [Impulse](/themes/impulse) **7.3.1**, a paid theme by Archetype Themes listed at $500 on the Shopify Theme Store with four presets (Impulse, Dune, Terrain and Apothecary). Impulse appeared on 8 of the 930 stores in the scan (rank 6 overall), and `lskd.co` is the only one of those 8 where the scan read a version number.

## Apps

The scan identified thirteen apps on `lskd.co` (with zero unresolved signatures), tied with [Fishwife](/teardowns/fishwife) for the highest count among curated stores:

- **Email & SMS:** [Klaviyo](/apps/klaviyo), [Alia](/apps/alia) and [Brevo PushOwl](/apps/pushowl)
- **Loyalty & Rewards:** [Rise Gift Cards & Store Credit](/apps/gift-card-loyalty-program) and [Rivo](/apps/rivo-loyalty)
- **Reviews:** [Yotpo Product Reviews App](/apps/yotpo)
- **Chat & Support:** [Gorgias](/apps/helpdesk)
- **Wishlist & Back in Stock:** [Amp Back in Stock & Preorder +](/apps/backinstock)
- **Shipping & Returns:** [Loop Returns & Exchanges](/apps/loop-returns)
- **Video & UGC:** [Tolstoy](/apps/tolstoy)
- **Search & Merchandising:** [Algolia AI Search & Discovery](/apps/algolia-search)
- **Affiliate & Referral:** [Rakuten Advertising](/apps/rakuten-advertising)
- **Privacy & Security:** [Pandectes GDPR Compliance](/apps/gdpr-cookie-consent)

> Thirteen resolved apps across ten categories — with overlap concentrated in email/SMS and loyalty.

Two categories carry multiple tools: three apps in Email & SMS (Klaviyo, Alia and PushOwl) and two in Loyalty & Rewards (Rise and Rivo). In practice, those pairs divide sub-jobs — gift cards and store credit versus points/VIP tiers, and email flows versus popups and push notifications.

## Tracking

Two tracking tags were detected: **Google Tag Manager** and **Triple Whale**, the latter handling ecommerce attribution alongside Rakuten on the affiliate side.

## What stands out in this stack

1. **Dedicated search over theme defaults:** [Algolia](/apps/algolia-search) appeared on just 4 of 930 scanned stores, making it an uncommon addition on top of Impulse.
2. **Shoppable video alongside reviews:** Pairing [Tolstoy](/apps/tolstoy) (14 of 930 stores) with [Yotpo](/apps/yotpo) (77 of 930; see [Loox vs Yotpo](/compare/loox-vs-yotpo) or [Judge.me vs Yotpo](/compare/judgeme-vs-yotpo)) splits video UGC from standard star ratings.
3. **Returns and support separation:** [Loop Returns](/apps/loop-returns) sits next to [Gorgias](/apps/helpdesk) rather than relying on a single helpdesk plugin for exchanges.
