> **How this was written.** From our 26 November 2026 crawl and captures (home, collection and
> product pages, desktop and mobile) of marinelayer.com. Apps are matched to sections from the
> page code; where an app could render in more than one place, we say so. No revenue or traffic
> estimates are used. Store counts are out of the 993 Shopify stores in our data.

## Theme

marinelayer.com reports its theme as "Black Friday 2026" (version 1.5.0), a name the brand has
given its seasonal build. It is not a Shopify Theme Store theme in our data, so we treat it as
custom. The page structure follows the usual Online Store 2.0 pattern.

## Apps

Our crawl identified nine apps on `marinelayer.com`:

- **Email & SMS:** [Klaviyo](/apps/klaviyo)
- **Support:** [Gorgias](/apps/helpdesk)
- **Upsell and personalisation:** [LimeSpot](/apps/limespot) and
  [Nosto](/apps/nosto-personalization-for-shopify)
- **Returns and exchanges:** [Loop Returns & Exchanges](/apps/loop-returns)
- **Affiliate and creators:** [Refersion](/apps/refersion) and [ShopMy](/apps/shopmy)
- **Testing and personalisation:** [Black Crow AI](/apps/black-crow-ai)
- **Cookie consent:** [Ketch](/apps/ketch)

## What stands out

1. **Two personalisation tools.** LimeSpot (7 of 993 stores) and Nosto (9 of 993) are both
   detected, which is uncommon.
2. **Two affiliate tools.** Refersion is on just 2 stores in our data; ShopMy is on 36.
3. **A short product page.** There is no reviews section on the captured product page; after
   the buy box come an image-with-text band and the sign-up form.
