> **Note.** Written from our 17 Nov 2026 scan of 930 Shopify stores (theme, detected apps, tracking tags) and Shopify App Store / Theme Store records. No revenue, traffic or conversion estimates are used.

## Theme

negativeunderwear.com runs [Dawn](/themes/dawn) **10.0.0**, Shopify’s free reference theme. Dawn is the most widely detected theme in our 930-store scan (20 stores, rank 1; compare with [Dawn vs Prestige](/compare/dawn-vs-prestige) or [Dawn vs Horizon](/compare/dawn-vs-horizon)), and `negativeunderwear.com` is the only one of those 20 where the scan read a version number.

## Apps

Instead of spending on a paid theme, the store runs twelve identified apps (zero unresolved detections) across eight categories:

- **Email & SMS:** [Klaviyo](/apps/klaviyo), [Alia](/apps/alia) and [AfterShip Email Marketing](/apps/automizely-messages)
- **Loyalty & Rewards:** [Okendo](/apps/okendo-reviews) and [Rise Gift Cards & Store Credit](/apps/gift-card-loyalty-program)
- **Chat & Support:** [Gorgias](/apps/helpdesk)
- **Testing & Personalization:** [Intelligems](/apps/intelligems)
- **Currency & Translation:** [Global-e CrossBorder](/apps/global-e-crossborder)
- **Affiliate & Referral:** [ShopMy Creator Affiliates](/apps/shopmy)
- **Privacy & Security:** [NoFraud](/apps/nofraud)
- **Payments & BNPL:** [Afterpay On-Site Messaging](/apps/afterpay-messaging)
- **Analytics:** [Polar Analytics](/apps/polar-analytics)

> Free theme, twelve specialized apps: the budget goes into experimentation, creator affiliates and cross-border tooling instead of theme licensing.

## Tracking

Two tracking tags were detected: **Google Ads** and **Google Tag Manager**, complemented on the analytics side by Polar Analytics inside the app layer.

## What stands out in this stack

1. **Free Dawn, premium experimentation:** Running [Intelligems](/apps/intelligems) (37 of 930 stores) on [Dawn](/themes/dawn) keeps template markup straightforward to split-test.
2. **Creator-led acquisition:** [ShopMy](/apps/shopmy) (28 of 930 stores) appears here just as on [Fishwife](/teardowns/fishwife), reflecting creator affiliate programs over traditional coupon networks.
3. **Overlapping email/SMS signals:** Detecting [Klaviyo](/apps/klaviyo), [Alia](/apps/alia) and [AfterShip Email Marketing](/apps/automizely-messages) together mirrors a common pattern where popups, lifecycle flows and shipping-triggered messages sit in separate tools.
