> **How this was written.** From our 17 Nov 2026 scan of 930 Shopify stores and our captures of
> 25 November 2026 (home, collection and product pages, desktop and mobile) of gravityblankets.com.
> Apps are matched to sections from the page code; where an app could render in more than one
> place, we say so. No revenue or traffic estimates are used.

## Theme

gravityblankets.com runs [Horizon](/themes/horizon), Shopify's newer free theme, detected on 14 of
the 930 stores in the scan. The home page has eleven sections on both desktop and mobile.

## Apps

Six apps were identified on `gravityblankets.com`:

- **Email & SMS:** [Klaviyo](/apps/klaviyo) and [Justuno](/apps/justuno)
- **Video & UGC:** [Instafeed](/apps/instafeed)
- **Upsell & Bundles:** [Rebuy](/apps/rebuy)
- **Reviews:** [Junip](/apps/junip)
- **Payments & BNPL:** [Klarna On-Site Messaging](/apps/klarnaosm)

## What stands out

1. **Proof before the footer.** Press logos, customer photos, reviews and an FAQ take up most of
   the lower home page: a pattern for a product people want reassurance about before buying.
2. **The same FAQ on two page types.** Collection and product pages both end with an FAQ block.
3. **A small, focused stack.** Six apps, each covering a different job, with no overlapping tools
   in the same category apart from email capture.
