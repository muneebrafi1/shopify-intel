> **How this was written.** From our 17 Nov 2026 scan of 930 Shopify stores and our captures of
> 25 November 2026 (home, collection and product pages, desktop and mobile) of saltandstone.com.
> Apps are matched to sections from the page code; where an app could render in more than one
> place, we say so. No revenue or traffic estimates are used.

## Theme

saltandstone.com runs [Dawn](/themes/dawn), Shopify's free reference theme. Dawn was detected on 20
of the 930 stores in the scan. The captured pages use Dawn's stock sections: announcement bar,
header, hero, featured collections, video, rich text, collection list and footer.

## Apps

The scan identified fourteen apps on `saltandstone.com`, more than three times the average of
about four per scanned store:

- **Email & SMS:** [Klaviyo](/apps/klaviyo), [Alia](/apps/alia) and
  [Powerful Contact Form Builder](/apps/form-builder-contact-form)
- **Reviews and loyalty:** [Okendo](/apps/okendo-reviews) and [Rivo](/apps/rivo-loyalty)
- **Upsell & Bundles:** [Rebuy](/apps/rebuy)
- **Shipping & Returns:** [Redo](/apps/redo)
- **Wishlist & Back in Stock:** [AMP Back in Stock](/apps/amp-back-in-stock)
- **Affiliate & Referral:** [ShopMy Creator Affiliates](/apps/shopmy)
- **Ads & Channels:** [AppLovin](/apps/applovin)
- **Testing & Personalization:** [Visually](/apps/visually-io) and [Black Crow AI](/apps/black-crow-ai)
- **Chat & Support:** [Richpanel](/apps/customer-support)
- **Privacy & Security:** [Osano Cookie Consent](/apps/osano-cmp)

## What stands out

1. **A free theme with a heavy stack.** Most of the work happens in apps, not in theme code: the
   page layout is stock Dawn, while testing, upsells, reviews and returns all come from apps.
2. **Two testing tools.** Visually (12 of 930 stores) and Black Crow AI (9 of 930) are both
   detected, which is rare in the scan.
3. **Short collection pages.** The collection page is just header, product grid and footer on
   both devices: no banner, no filters band above the grid.
