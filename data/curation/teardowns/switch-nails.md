> **How this was written.** From our 26 November 2026 crawl and captures (home, collection and
> product pages, desktop and mobile) of switch-nails.com. Apps are matched to sections from the
> page code; where an app could render in more than one place, we say so. No revenue or traffic
> estimates are used. Store counts are out of the 993 Shopify stores in our data.

## Theme

switch-nails.com reports its theme as "Revolution", which is not a Shopify Theme Store theme in
our data, so we treat it as custom.

## Apps

Our crawl identified seven apps on `switch-nails.com`:

- **Email & SMS:** [Klaviyo](/apps/klaviyo)
- **Reviews:** [Judge.me](/apps/judgeme)
- **Bundles and gifts:** [BOGOS](/apps/freegifts)
- **Social proof:** [Instafeed](/apps/instafeed)
- **Translation and currency:** [T Lab](/apps/content-translation) and
  [Hextom AI Translate & Currency](/apps/translate-my-store)
- **Cookie consent:** [Pandectes GDPR Compliance](/apps/gdpr-cookie-consent)

## What stands out

1. **Products first.** Five featured-collection rows make up most of the home page.
2. **Two translation apps.** T Lab and Hextom AI Translate & Currency are each detected on 10 of
   the 993 stores, and both run here.
3. **Upsell rows around trust badges.** The product page puts three upsell rows around the trust
   band before reviews appear.
