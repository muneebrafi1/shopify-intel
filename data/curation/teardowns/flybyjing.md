> **How this was written.** From our 17 Nov 2026 scan of 930 Shopify stores and our captures of
> 25 November 2026 (home, collection and product pages, desktop and mobile) of flybyjing.com. Apps
> are matched to sections from the page code; where an app could render in more than one place, we
> say so. No revenue or traffic estimates are used.

## Theme

flybyjing.com runs [Shapes](/themes/shapes) **3.0.4**, a paid theme by Switch listed at $420 on the
Shopify Theme Store, detected on 6 of the 930 stores in the scan.

## Apps

Seven apps were identified on `flybyjing.com`:

- **Email & SMS:** [Klaviyo](/apps/klaviyo)
- **Chat & Support:** [Gorgias](/apps/helpdesk)
- **Reviews and loyalty:** [Okendo](/apps/okendo-reviews)
- **Upsell & Bundles:** [Rebuy](/apps/rebuy)
- **Wishlist & Back in Stock:** [AMP Back in Stock](/apps/amp-back-in-stock)
- **Subscriptions:** [Inveterate by Onward](/apps/inveterate)
- **Privacy & Security:** [Osano Cookie Consent](/apps/osano-cmp)

## What stands out

1. **Home page as a billboard.** With a single hero between header and footer, the home page
   sends visitors on rather than selling directly.
2. **A rare membership app.** Inveterate (2 of 930 stores) adds a paid membership programme, one
   of the least common apps in the scan.
3. **Same stack shape as Salt & Stone.** Klaviyo, Okendo, Rebuy, AMP and Osano all appear on both
   [Fly By Jing](/stores/flybyjing.com) and [Salt & Stone](/teardowns/saltandstone).
